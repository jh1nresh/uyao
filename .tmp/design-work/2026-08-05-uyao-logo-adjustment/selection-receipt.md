- Decision date: 2026-08-05
- Decision owner: founder
- Selected: Hybrid — Candidate B structure + Candidate A corner discipline
- Rejected: Candidate A's detached diagonal and cold infrastructure tone; Candidate B's overly generic rounded finish
- Reason: preserve B's connected, stable silhouette while using A's squared outer construction; increase friendliness through internal joins and authored wordmark rather than candy-like rounding
